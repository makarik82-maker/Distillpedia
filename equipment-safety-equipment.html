🧯 Safety Equipment

The non-negotiable kit: fire protection, ventilation, personal protection and the rules that keep a distillation run safe.

🛑 These items are mandatory. Alcohol vapor is flammable and a blocked still is a pressure bomb — safety gear and correct setup are not optional.

Mandatory kit

  • Fire extinguisher: dry chemical or CO₂, within arm's reach of the still.
  • Ventilation: open windows, exhaust fan, or outdoor setup; never distil in a sealed room.
  • Heat-resistant gloves & safety glasses: protect against hot surfaces, steam and splashes.
  • First aid kit: burns and cuts are the most common injuries.

Setup rules

  • Never seal a still: always keep an open vapor path; a blocked output builds explosive pressure.
  • Food-grade materials only: copper, stainless 304/316, borosilicate glass, food-grade silicone. No lead solder, no automotive radiators.
  • Check seals: smell and listen for hissing before and during the run; stop immediately on a leak.
  • Never leave a running still unattended.

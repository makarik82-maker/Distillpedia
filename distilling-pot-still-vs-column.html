⚗️ Pot Still vs. Column

Choosing hardware for the spirit you want: keep the raw material's character, or chase neutral purity.

Flavor spirits → pot still

  • Fruit, berry and grain distillates need a pot still (or a column in pot still mode) to keep aroma and taste.
  • A fully assembled reflux column strips the character, leaving only alcohol taste — not what you want from cherries or barley.
  • Add a section of copper packing to remove sulfur compounds; stainless packing only slows the run.

Neutral spirit → column

  • For vodka, tinctures and gin bases, a reflux/rectification column gives clean, high-proof spirit.
  • Columns are also the right tool for processing "tails" and low-quality washes.
  • Remember: never seal the still; keep the vapor path open at all times.

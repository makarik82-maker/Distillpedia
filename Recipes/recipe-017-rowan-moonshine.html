🍒 Rowan Moonshine (Black Chokeberry or Red Berries)

Rowan moonshine has a light woody aroma and original taste with notes of astringent tannins, dry grass, and almonds. Red berries give pronounced bitterness, while black chokeberry gives more fruity, wine, and chocolate tones. This is a universal recipe suitable for both red and black berries since their properties are similar.

Category
Fruit
ABV
40–45%
Difficulty
Advanced
Fermentation
5–50 days
Yield
~10 kg rowan
⚠️ Legal & Safety Disclaimer: This recipe is provided for educational purposes only. Distilling alcohol at home is illegal in many countries without a licence. Always check your local laws. Read our Distillation Safety Guide before attempting any distillation.

Ingredients

  • 10 kg rowan berries — red or black chokeberry
  • Sugar: 5 kg (500 g per 1 kg berries)
  • Water: 25 L (5 L per 1 kg sugar)
  • Wine or spirit yeast (optional): for 40 L wash

Baker's yeast: 20 g dry or 100 g pressed per 1 kg sugar. Rowan contains 5–8% natural sugar (usually 6–7%). Theoretical yield: 60–96 ml of 40% moonshine per 1 kg berries, but practical yield is 14–20% lower.

🛑 Important: Collect rowan after the first frost if possible — low temperature partially converts starch to simple sugars and breaks down pectin (which yeast converts to methanol). You can simulate frost by freezing berries for 12 hours. Remove stems and leaves (they increase bitterness). Always discard the "heads" fraction. See Distillation Safety Guide.

Step-by-Step Process

1
Prepare the berries. Remove leaves and stems. If fermenting on wild yeast, do not wash the rowan.
2
Crush the berries. Mash by hand or other method, trying not to damage most seeds — otherwise the finished moonshine will have pronounced almond taste. There's no need to grind to a paste — just crushing is enough.
3
Place in container. Put the rowan in a plastic, enameled, or stainless container with a wide neck — pot or bucket.
4
Make the sugar syrup. Dissolve sugar in water. Can heat to 30 °C, but no higher.
5
Combine and stir. Add the syrup to the berry mass. Stir until homogeneous.
6
Add yeast. Prepare yeast according to package instructions, then add to the wash.
7
Initial fermentation (combined scheme). Tie the container neck with cheesecloth to keep insects out. Leave the rowan wash for 4 days in a dark room with temperature 20–26 °C. Stir every 8–12 hours, sinking floating berry residues. Fermentation signs should appear within 36 hours: foam, hissing, and characteristic sour smell.
8
Filter the wash. Strain through a fine sieve or several layers of cheesecloth, squeezing the pulp dry (no longer needed). This "combined" scheme extracts aromatic and flavor substances from the rowan without oversaturating the moonshine with tannins that give too harsh an astringent taste.
9
Continue fermentation. Pour the wash into the fermentation vessel, filling up to 80% of volume. Install an airlock. Leave at 18–28 °C in a dark room or cover with dense fabric.
10
Confirm fermentation is complete. This takes 5–50 days depending on yeast and temperature. Signs: airlock bubbles fewer than 5 times per minute, foam almost gone, sediment at bottom, smells like alcohol and tastes dry (not sweet).
11
Transfer to the still. Siphon the finished wash off sediment through a tube, trying not to disturb the sediment. Fill the still 30–70% of volume.
12
Assemble the equipment. Set up in pot still mode: column height up to 60 cm, no packing, leave only direct-flow cooler (disable dephlegmator) and optional thump keg.
13
First distillation. Distill at maximum speed: distillate may come out warm and slightly cloudy, but must be without steam. Stop when ABV drops below 25%, or distill almost to zero if energy costs seem justified.
14
Measure and dilute. Determine ABV at 20 °C and calculate total pure alcohol. Dilute to 18–20%. Do not clean with charcoal, milk, or other methods — preserve the rowan aroma and taste.
15
Second distillation with cuts. After boiling, reduce heat so the moonshine drips slowly and stays cool. Collect the first 12–14% of pure alcohol volume as "heads" — discard this fraction, never consume it.
16
Collect the hearts. Smoothly increase heating power: moonshine should drip quickly or flow in a thin stream, but remain cool. Collect the "hearts" until alcohol concentration drops below 43%. Then stop or collect "tails" separately.
17
Dilute and rest. Dilute the rowan moonshine with softened water to drinking strength — 40–45%. Transfer to glass jars or bottles, seal tightly, and leave for at least 3 days in a cool place to stabilize. Shelf life is unlimited.
💡 Editor's tip: Do not mix black chokeberry and red rowan in one wash — the moonshine will be "blurred" without pronounced tones. Blend finished distillates instead. Sugar is recommended for rowan (unlike most fruits) to reduce excessive astringency and bitterness.

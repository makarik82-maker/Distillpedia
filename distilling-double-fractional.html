🔁 Double Fractional Distillation

Why two runs with fraction separation is the single most important step — it removes harmful impurities and smooths the spirit.

One run gives you raw, harsh spirit loaded with impurities. Two runs with proper cuts turn it into a clean, drinkable distillate. Double fractional distillation can compensate for almost every other mistake in the process.

First run (stripping)

  • Run fast, without fraction separation; collect until stream strength falls below ~25% (or "to water" if you accept the energy cost).
  • Goal: separate alcohol and water from solids and heavy compounds.

Dilution between runs

  • Dilute the first-run spirit to 18–20% ABV before the second run — lower strength improves separation and reduces fire risk.
  • Calculate pure alcohol first: volume (L) × ABV (%) ÷ 100.

Second run (fractional)

  1. Heads: collect the first 8–15% of pure alcohol (higher for high-pectin fruit) and discard — it contains methanol and acetone.
  2. Hearts: collect the drinking fraction until stream strength falls to ~40–45%.
  3. Tails: stop, or collect separately for later rectification.
